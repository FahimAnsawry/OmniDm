- New: download videos from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X.
- New: a post with several videos lets you choose which one to download.
- Posts that need you to be logged in (often on Instagram and Facebook) work from the Download button in your browser.
- Facebook and Instagram often send the picture and the sound separately; for now you get two files, the video and "(sound).m4a".
- Error messages now name the site, for example "TikTok: This video is private".

Update the browser extension too:
1. Go to the OmniDM 0.2.0 release page on GitHub and download OmniDM-Extension-Chrome-0.2.0.zip (Chrome, Edge, Brave) or OmniDM-Extension-Firefox-0.2.0.zip.
2. Unzip it into your OmniDM_chrome (or OmniDM_firefox) folder, replacing what's there.
3. On your browser's extensions page, click the reload arrow ↻ on OmniDM.
